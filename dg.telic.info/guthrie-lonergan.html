Guthrie Lonergan
exhibition: march 2009

We asked Guthrie Lonergan to produce the March 2009 exhibition at the Distributed Gallery. He chose to curate works by artists Charles Broskoski, Aleksandra Domanovic, Travis Hallenbeck, and Joel Holmberg. In sympathy with the exhibition, Guthrie is leading a Stock footage workshop at The Public School.
