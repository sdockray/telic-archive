Accretion in Two Steps
curated by James Merle Thomas. april 2009

We asked James Merle Thomas to produce the April 2009 Distributed Gallery exhibition. He chose to curate works by artists Lucy Raven and Chen Shaoxiong. The exhibition will conclude on April 24th with an outdoor screening of short videos by Chen Shaoxiong, followed by Lucy Raven's China Town (2008).
