Tamala Poljak and Anna Oxygen on KPFK

Tamala Poljak and Anna Oxygen, who have organized Conversations That Never Happened (June 28 - July19) will be on Feminist Magazine on KPFK, Wednesday, June 25 at 7pm. Among other things, they’ll be speaking about the exhibition at TELIC. We’ll try and post a recording of the program, but we can’t promise anything so listen on 90.7 (Los Angeles) or 98.7 (Santa Barbara)!
