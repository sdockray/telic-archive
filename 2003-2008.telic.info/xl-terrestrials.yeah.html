The XL Terrestrials present “The Transmigration of Cinema”

March 30, 2007 at 7:00 pm to 10:00 pm
You need to upgrade or install Adobe Flash Player

A screening program ranging from art flix to mainstream movies to
guerrilla media to ubiquitous online effluvium, all presented in an open
forum theater as an interactive and self-diagnostic application that will
tell us if we are still connected to human consciousness, and how might we
still access a customized and liveable “Operating System” based on
un-programmed desires, community and individuation.

The XL Terrestrials, a team of unlicensed psycho-media analysts and
illegitimate art practitioners from San Francisco, Berlin and beyond,
present this cinematic, theatrical and ecological examination of movies,
mass media, the internet, and You, the human species, in the midst of an
epic-scale virtual migration.

Recently back from their eastern Europe and Balkan region research
expedition, the XL Terrestrials bring you an incredible collection of “no
border” cinema in combination with strategies for disengaging the “OS -
Spectacle 2.0″ in times of war, hypermarkets, and virtual cul de sacs.
